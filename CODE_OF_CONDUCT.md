# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Welcome different perspectives and technical experience levels.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, never on people.
- Accept maintainer decisions and ask questions in good faith.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and disruptive or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's Security or contact channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project healthy.
